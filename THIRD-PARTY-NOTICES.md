## Standalone support effects and spectral fur

`meshes/FightWithMe/Support/` contains original protective ribs, recovery wisps, targeting
marks, leg bindings, rally lights, wind pulses, impact streaks, level-up light, flame tongues/embers, steel highlights and spirit
weapon trails. `tools/fx/BuildSupport.js` creates their geometry from scratch. They reference
stock Skyrim effect textures, never meshes or textures from external spell/effect mods.
`include/FWM/SupportArt.h` and `src/Effects.cpp` animate and fade these temporary models.

`textures/FightWithMe/SpectralFur.dds` is an original procedural strand-density texture made by
`tools/fx/BuildFur.js`, including nine mip levels. The furred wolf and bear variants use this
texture on Fight With Me's generated shell geometry; no Fluffworks assets are read or bundled.
Skyrim remains the source of the underlying wolf/bear body geometry and skin textures.

The previous Triumvirate, Elemental Destruction Magic Redux and Vibrant Weapons integrations
have been removed. Their original assets are not distributed in either download. Skyrim-derived
models remain intended for use with Skyrim, as described above.

Healing Circle's `HealingSeal.dds` and `HealingPulse.dds` were also generated for this

Elemental Burst's `textures/FightWithMe/ElementalIce.dds` was generated for this project
with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. Its original artwork and generation prompt

Sunder's `textures/FightWithMe/SunderMetal.dds` was also generated for this project with

## Class skill-tree backdrops

Eighteen original transparent illustrations for Fight With Me, generated with the built-in image generation tool. Vanilla uses gouache portraits; Oxblood uses Nordic woodcut portraits; Nordic uses manuscript ink-and-wash weapons and spell artifacts. No extracted game artwork or third-party mod images are used in these backdrops. Generation prompts and source records are retained in tools/ui-preview/backdrop-redesign-prompts.json. The PNG files ship in the separate Effects pack under interface/FightWithMe/backdrops. Background particle animation is original procedural UI code.


Azura's Moon: Wispmother geometry comes from Skyrim's `meshes/actors/wisp/character assets/wisp.nif`; Azura's Star comes from `meshes/clutter/daedric/azurastar.nif` (Bethesda Softworks). `tools/azurasmoon/BuildMoon.js` reposes the stock weighted skin into a prayer and builds original celestial glow, wave and aura geometry. Animation and blessing mechanics are original Fight With Me work. These assets are for Skyrim under Bethesda's terms for game mods. Only installed stock texture paths are referenced; no third-party mod textures are bundled. The preview can be regenerated with `--moon-only --stock-textures`.

Skald: spectral lute geometry derives from Skyrim's AnimObjectLute (Bethesda Softworks).
The four Skald song WAVs are faster, pitch-preserved excerpts of the installed game's
mus_bardlute_01–04 recordings, made by tools/skald/ArrangeLute.py; the Single Note's
SkaldNote.wav is a 0.8-second excerpt of mus_bardlute_03 made by tools/skald/BuildNote.py.
Source and output hashes are in tools/skald/arrangements.json and note-audio.json. These
derived assets are intended for use with Skyrim. Wave and contact geometry and animation
are original project work, referencing installed Skyrim effect textures.
The Skald's three class backdrops (`interface/FightWithMe/backdrops/*-skald.png`) and its
four effect texture atlases (`textures/FightWithMe/Skald/*Atlas.dds`) are original
illustrations generated with the built-in image generation tool; their prompts are retained
in tools/skald/theme-art-prompts.json and generated-fx-prompts.json. The Skald's meshes,
textures, sounds and backdrops ship in the Path of the Companions download.
