## Required ability FX sources (not bundled)

Fight With Me references models from the following separately installed mods. It creates
its own temporary art objects pointing to those model paths; no meshes, textures, scripts,
plugins or source spells from these mods are included in this package.

- **Triumvirate - Mage Archetypes**, Enai Siaion: support auras, barriers, healing,
  targeting marks, bindings and magical buffs.
  https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/39170
- **Elemental Destruction Magic Redux**, original mod and assets by reaperix;
  Redux edition maintained/uploaded by phenderix: earth impacts, wind bursts and cloaks,
  and water cloak art.
  https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/37211
- **Vibrant Weapons - Fire Frost Shock**, jg1: fire, shock, frost and combined
  fire/shock weapon particle models, selected for the equipped weapon and hand.
  https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/40087

These credits do not relicense the original assets. Other contributors' credits and
permissions remain with each source mod. Fight With Me does not use Triumvirate's
third-party creature models or redistribute Vibrant Weapons' other assets.
